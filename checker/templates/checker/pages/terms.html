What the service is

Carverse Check is a free information service, run by Sumit Yadav as an independent project. It shows a vehicle's MOT history as published by DVSA, points out patterns in it, and compares the vehicle's model with others of the same age.

It is not advice to buy or not buy a vehicle, and it isn't a substitute for an inspection by a qualified mechanic or a full vehicle history check.

Accuracy

  • MOT records come from DVSA and can contain mistakes made when they were entered. We show them as DVSA holds them.
  • Our findings are automated readings of those records. A finding is a reason to ask a question, not proof that anything is wrong.
  • Comparisons describe groups of vehicles, not the vehicle you're checking. The methodology explains how they're made and their limits.

Fair use

You may use Carverse Check to check vehicles for your own purposes. Please don't:

  • run automated or bulk lookups, or copy reports at scale
  • try to get round any limits, or interfere with how the site works
  • use the service to harass anyone or for anything unlawful

DVSA limits how often its service can be used. We may block traffic that would put the site over those limits.
