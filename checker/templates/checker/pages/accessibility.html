Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site hasn't been audited by an independent accessibility specialist, so we can't yet claim it fully does.

What we've done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels, so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Text and background colours are chosen for strong contrast.
  • Charts have text descriptions, and every number in a chart is also given in words nearby.
  • Colour is never the only way information is shown: findings also carry labels such as "SERIOUS".
  • Animations are switched off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The site works without JavaScript, and on small screens and when zoomed in.

Known limitations

  • In the chart comparing models (the funnel plot), individual dots can't be explored with a screen reader. The chart's description and the text above it give its main points.
  • The site uses a dark colour scheme only. A light option isn't available yet.
  • MOT defect descriptions come from DVSA as written, and some use technical wording.

Report a problem

If something doesn't work for you, please email {{ contact_email }} with the page address and what happened. We aim to reply within five working days, and we'll tell you how and when we'll fix it.
