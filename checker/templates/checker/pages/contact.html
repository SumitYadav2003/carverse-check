Carverse Check is run by one person, so replies can take a few working days.

Helpful things to include

  • The address of the page you were on (copy it from your browser).
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • For a report that looks wrong, the number plate and which finding you mean.

Before you write

  • A mistake in an MOT record (a wrong mileage, say) has to be corrected by the test centre that did the test, through DVSA. We show records as DVSA holds them.
  • A question about a particular car is best asked of the seller. The How it works page explains what each finding means.
  • Many questions are answered in Help and FAQs.
